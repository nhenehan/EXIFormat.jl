Thanks

EXI is a careful, clearly written W3C Recommendation with good open libraries behind it, and that work made this project possible.

  • Rumen Kyusakov wrote exip, a compact C implementation of EXI, and co-edited the Second Edition of the EXI specification. exip's decoder is one of the checks EXIFormat's output must pass.
  • Daniel Peintner wrote EXIficient with Richard Kuntschke at Siemens, co-edited the Second Edition, and edited the EXI Primer. EXIFormat's bytes are checked against EXIficient's.
  • Takuki Kamiya edited the First Edition, chaired the EXI Working Group, and wrote Nagasena (OpenEXI). John Schneider also edited the First Edition, and Santiago Pericas-Geertsen co-edited the Primer and wrote the W3C test framework.
  • The W3C EXI Working Group as a whole made the specification that EXIFormat follows closely.

Thank you.
